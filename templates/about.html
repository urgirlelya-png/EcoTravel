A carbon footprint is the amount of CO2 emitted by a trip. It depends on transport type and distance. A plane emits about 250 g CO2 per passenger-km, a car about 180 g, and a train about 40 g.

The rating is based on total CO2 emissions:

A up to 20 kg CO2 - excellent
B 20-60 kg CO2 - good
C 60-150 kg CO2 - acceptable
D 150-400 kg CO2 - needs improvement
F over 400 kg CO2 - critical

The app uses the OpenWeatherMap API. Two requests are made: one for current weather and another for air quality data (AQI). This lets us assess climate and environmental conditions at the destination.
